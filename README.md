# Sakchyam Bastakoti
## AI & Embedded Systems Engineer

I am an AI and machine learning enthusiast with a strong passion for robotics, IoT, and embedded systems. My focus is on developing intelligent, real-world solutions through seamless integration of hardware and software.

I have practical experience with microcontrollers, including **ESP32**, **ESP8266**, **Arduino**, and **Raspberry Pi**, and have developed systems that integrate artificial intelligence, computer vision, sensors, and automation. My portfolio includes projects in humanoid robotics, face recognition, emotion detection, optical character recognition (OCR) systems, and IoT-based smart solutions.

I am proficient in **Python**, **C**, and **C++**, and I enjoy designing scalable, production-oriented systems rather than just prototypes. I actively explore emerging technologies in AI-driven robotics, automation, and ethical hacking, and I strongly believe in the importance of continuous learning and open-source collaboration.

My goal is to grow as a full-stack hardware–software developer and contribute to impactful projects in AI, robotics, and smart technologies that solve real-world problems.
